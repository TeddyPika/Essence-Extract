03 / an engineering workflow, not a prompt pile

Evidence in.
Useful artifact out.

Skills inspect relevant files, keep uncertainty visible, and return a reusable shape. They do not silently turn a hunch into a fact.

Inspect the source

Use repository tools or the supplied incident, decision, and product materials—not a guess about what might be there.

Separate fact from inference

Every claim has a source location. Assumptions and residual risks get their own fields, not a footnote.

Ship a reusable shape

Embedded structured data can feed issue templates, PR notes, postmortems, and your own lightweight automation.
